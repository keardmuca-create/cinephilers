# **App Name**: Cinephilers

## Core Features:

- User Authentication & Profile Management: Allows users to sign up, log in securely, create and manage their personal profiles, including photo, bio, and tracking following/followers.
- Movie & Show Discovery: Enables users to explore films and TV shows through curated sections (Today's Pick, Featured, Top 10), genre-based browsing, Coming Soon lists, and a global search bar.
- Detailed Content Information: Provides comprehensive pages for each movie or show, displaying details like title, year, genre, watch trailer, cast, crew, photos, community reviews, quotes, and trivia.
- Personal Watch History & Ratings: Users can mark content as watched, add to a watchlist, submit personal ratings, and create custom lists. Includes a profile section showing watched, rated, and custom lists along with rating analytics.
- Community Social Feed: Displays a personalized activity feed of friends' interactions (e.g., watched, rated, reviewed), allows liking and commenting on posts, and viewing user profiles and their reviews.
- AI-Powered Personalized Recommendation Tool: A generative AI tool that curates a 'Top Picks For You' row based on a user's viewing history, ratings, and genre preferences, helping them discover new content.
- Friend Network & Chat: Facilitates friend management, including searching for and adding other users, viewing their activity, and engaging in real-time direct chat conversations.

## Style Guidelines:

- Overall dark color scheme to evoke a cinematic experience, emphasizing the content on screen.
- Primary interactive color: A rich, deep plum (#58248C) used for buttons, active elements, and accentuating user interactions against the dark background.
- Background color: A very dark, subtle purplish-gray (#211A22) providing a sophisticated and deep visual canvas for all content.
- Accent color: A vibrant electric blue (#2E2EE6) for highlights, critical calls-to-action like 'Watch Trailer', and creating bright cinematic contrast.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for titles and prominent headings, offering a modern, slightly technical, and bold appearance fitting for digital media.
- Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for all body text, ensuring excellent readability, neutrality, and clean aesthetics for descriptions, reviews, and general information.
- Use sleek, minimalist, line-art icons that align with a modern cinematic aesthetic for navigation and functional elements (e.g., play button, heart icon, share icon).
- Content-focused layout with generous spacing, enabling full-bleed visuals for movie posters and trailers, providing an immersive viewing experience on dark backgrounds.
- Subtle, smooth transition animations for page changes, content loading (e.g., card fade-ins), and interactive elements (e.g., button hovers) to enhance the fluidity and premium feel of the app.
